# Legal & safety notes

This project is an **unofficial, unaffiliated** WhatsApp client. It is not associated with,
authorized by, or endorsed by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc. "WhatsApp" is a trademark of
its respective owner.

## Terms of Service

Using a third-party client to connect to WhatsApp **violates the WhatsApp Terms of Service**.
WhatsApp actively detects unofficial clients and **bans accounts** that use them. Bans are often
permanent and tied to the phone number.

**Use a burner number. Never link an account you depend on.**

## Prohibited uses

This software is intended for personal, technical, single-user use on hardware you own. It must
**not** be used for:

- bulk / automated / unsolicited messaging (spam),
- scraping or harvesting data about other users,
- monitoring or tracking any person without their knowledge (stalkerware),
- evasion of account bans or security controls.

## Calls

No community library implements WhatsApp voice/video calling. This client cannot place or receive
calls. (Accepted for the target deployment, where WhatsApp calling is unavailable anyway.)

Provided as-is, with no warranty. Jailbreaking, sideloading, and running an unofficial client can
result in data loss, account loss, or device instability. You accept that risk by using this.
